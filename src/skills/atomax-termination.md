# Atomax termination diagrams

How Atomax-UK draws termination / connection diagrams. Draft from our general practice, to be finished from Tom's termination examples. Use with the Atomax CAD house rules.

## What a termination diagram shows
- Each panel / JB terminal rail with every terminal in order, its number and what lands on it: cable number, core number or colour, and the far-end device and terminal.
- Cables come in from the side with their cable number, cores x size and type, then fan out to their terminals; screens and earths to their own terminals or the earth bar.
- Spare cores shown and labelled SPARE, parked on spare terminals or the earth bar as the job requires.

## Rules
- Terminal numbers, core numbers and cable numbers exactly as on the IO list / cable schedule / loop drawings. Never invent a terminal.
- Every cable on the BCD for that panel / JB appears, and nothing else.
- Text height 2, cores never crossing each other on the way to the rail.
- Notes: terminals identified to BS EN 60445; screens earthed at one end only unless noted; cables glanded and earthed per the house notes.
